03 — Approach

What I optimize for

a.

Bold at a glance

One clear idea per design, readable at arm’s length — a window sticker from the sidewalk, a label from the shelf, a badge from across the room.

b.

Built to customize

The name, trade, flavor, or team is the point of a template. Placeholder text sits exactly where a buyer’s words belong, in type that’s easy to replace.

c.

Clean, original files

Simple vector shapes drawn from scratch, tidy artboards at print-friendly sizes, and no stock, traced, or licensed elements to untangle later.
